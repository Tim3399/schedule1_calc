# Release 1.7.0

## A Schedule I look for the calculator

The calculator gets its own visual identity in the spirit of Schedule I. An own
"Best Mix for Schedule I" wordmark sits over a drawn low-poly scene band, and the
calculator itself sits on a screen with a beige rim. Form and result stand side by
side as two equally high columns from 900 px; below that they stack. Barlow is
self-hosted with its OFL licence, so no external font service is contacted.

The theme switch offers **System**, **Dusk** and **Night**. System follows the
colour scheme of the operating system; an explicit choice still wins.

## Results that read the same on every tab

Find Best Mix, Your Recipe and Match Effects show their results on the same
surface: profit as a green banknote, money as `$70.00`, the mix as tiles with base
product and step prices, and effects as neutral pills with the game colour as a dot.
A running search shows a calm progress bar that no longer restarts on every update.
A stopped search carries its own **Search stopped — optimality not proven** badge.
The result without JavaScript uses the same texts.

Fields and selects with a rejected value show an inline error message and a red
error state that assistive technology reads together with the field.

## Accessibility

Every control has a target of at least 44 px and is reachable by keyboard with a
visible focus ring. Text, theme switch labels in every state and form placeholders
reach a contrast of at least 4.5:1 in Dusk and Night. Increased-contrast settings
are honoured, and the profit count-up and result fade-in are off under reduced
motion. With large text on narrow screens the theme switch wraps into equal-width
rows instead of clipping its labels. Contrast and reduced motion are covered by
tests.

## What stays the same

The calculation runs in the browser as before. The private server API, the game
data, the search modes and limits are unchanged, and the form fields keep their
names. The stored theme choice keeps its values: a light or dark choice saved with
1.6.0 shows up as Dusk or Night.

## Verification

Run on `release/1.7.0` on Windows: `tools/project.py check-format`, `check` and
`test` (221 tests) pass, `node --test tests/browser/*.test.cjs` passes 147 of 147,
and `tools/project.py start` reports `[web] Ready` with `version=1.7.0`. Linux and
the container are checked by the release pipeline. The full list of changes is in
the [changelog](../../CHANGELOG.md).
